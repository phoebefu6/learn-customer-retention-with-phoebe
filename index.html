Learn Customer Retention with Phoebe

Two tracks from "our retention is 27%" to knowing what that means and what to do about it

A stakeholder track that teaches why retention is the money metric, how to read a cohort heatmap in thirty seconds, which levers actually move the number - with the famous case studies folklore-checked - and exactly what to demand from your data team. And an analyst track that builds the whole toolkit in Python on real and synthetic data: D1/7/14/30 retention defined precisely (three different "D7"s exist, and tools disagree on purpose), the registration-cohort triangle and its heatmap, the viz gallery, RFM segmentation, survival analysis, CLV models, and churn prediction that actually helps. One law governs every number in the course: cohort-based, forward-looking, frozen once complete - a completed month's retention never restates, only the current window updates. Ecommerce and game examples throughout. A live in-browser cohort simulator lets you flatten the curve lever by lever and discover what doubling ad spend cannot buy.
